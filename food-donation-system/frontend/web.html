The Crisis

One third of all food produced is lost or wasted.

While restaurants, event halls, and households throw away fresh, edible food, millions go to bed hungry. This isn't just a food crisis; it's a logistics crisis.

Our platform utilizes cutting-edge AI and real-time mapping to instantly connect surplus food with local NGOs and volunteers. We ensure that good food reaches an empty plate, not a landfill.
